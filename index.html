Private online tools that work on your device

Most free online tools work by uploading your file to a company's servers, processing it there, and sending it back. That means your contracts, IDs, tax forms and family photos sit — at least briefly — on someone else's computer. PrivyForge takes a different approach: the processing code is downloaded to your browser and runs on your own device, using JavaScript and WebAssembly builds of well-known open-source libraries. Your files are never transmitted.

You can check this yourself: load any tool, switch off your Wi-Fi, and use it. It still works.

How it works

  1. Open a tool. The page downloads the code it needs, once. Your browser caches it, so the next visit is faster and works offline.
  2. Add your file or numbers. Drop files on the box, click to choose them, or paste. Calculators update as you type.
  3. Download the result. It's created in your browser's memory and saved straight to your device.

What you can do here

  • PDF tools: merge several PDFs into one, split out or reorder pages, and compress large PDFs for email and upload portals.
  • Image tools: compress JPG, PNG, WebP and AVIF images, convert between formats, and resize to a maximum width or height — in batches, downloaded as a ZIP.
  • Money tools: mortgage, loan, refinance and rent-vs-buy calculators, a debt payoff planner, and a subscription finder that reads a bank CSV on your device.
  • Utilities: a QR code generator whose codes never expire, and a file hash checker to verify downloads.

A quick example

Say a job portal wants a single PDF under 2 MB, and you have a scanned cover letter, a résumé and a references page. Drop all three into Merge PDF, drag them into order and download one file. If it's over the limit, open Compress PDF and choose "Recommended", or "Smallest" for strict caps. Neither step sends the documents anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is PrivyForge really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no sign-up, no daily limits and no watermarks.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Files are read into your browser's memory and processed on your device. The only things downloaded from our site are the pages and the tool code.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any current mobile browser. Very large files are limited by your phone's memory, so a laptop is faster for big batches.

Is there a size limit?

Most tools have no fixed cap; the practical limit is your device's memory and processor. The file hash checker accepts files up to 2 GB.

    Compression level

    How to compress a PDF

    1. Add your PDF(s).
    2. Pick a level. "Recommended" suits most scanned documents. Use "Smallest" for portals with a 1–2 MB cap.
    3. Download. If a file can't get smaller, you get the original back — never a bigger file.

    Which level should I use?

    Recommended and Smallest re-render each page as an optimized image. This is the most effective way to shrink scanned documents and photo-heavy PDFs, often by 50–90%. The trade-off: text in the result is no longer selectable or searchable.

    Lossless rebuilds the PDF's internal structure without touching content, so text stays selectable. Savings are smaller (typically 0–20%) and depend on how the PDF was made.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. Everything runs in your browser using pdf.js and pdf-lib. Your documents never leave your device.

    Why can't I select text after compressing?

    The Recommended and Smallest levels convert pages into images to get big savings. Choose Lossless if you need selectable text.

    How do I get a PDF under 1 MB?

    Use "Smallest". If it's still too big, split the PDF and send the parts separately using our Split PDF tool.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026.

1. Your files and numbers

PrivyForge tools — image and PDF tools, the QR code generator, the file hash checker, the subscription finder and the calculators — run entirely in your web browser. Files you open and figures you enter are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us. We cannot see, store or recover them. You can confirm this by loading a tool, disconnecting from the internet, and using it.

2. Hosting

PrivyForge is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, Cloudflare processes standard request data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. Cloudflare handles this data under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. We do not receive a copy of your IP address.

3. Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits in aggregate (for example, how many people viewed a page and which country they came from). According to Cloudflare, it does not use cookies or localStorage and does not fingerprint visitors. It never sees the contents of files or the numbers you type into a tool.

4. Affiliate links

Some pages include links to products and services we think are useful, marked "Affiliate links". If you click one and sign up or buy, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Those partners don't pay to change our tools' results, and every calculator works the same whether or not you click. When you leave PrivyForge through an affiliate link, the partner's site may set its own cookies to credit the referral, and its privacy policy applies there.

5. Data stored on your device

PrivyForge stores a few things in your own browser: cached copies of the tool code (so tools load fast and work offline), and — if you use them — favorites, recently used tools, saved calculator scenarios and checkup progress on the My PrivyForge page. This data never leaves your browser. You can delete it from My PrivyForge ("Clear everything") or by clearing site data in your browser.

6. Children

PrivyForge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access or delete personal information, or to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don't run accounts and never receive your files, so we hold very little about you. To make a request, contact us.

8. Changes

If our practices change, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

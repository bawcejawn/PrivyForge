[iLovePDF](https://www.ilovepdf.com/) is one of the most popular PDF websites, with a long list of tools. Its free plan works well for occasional use, but the files you process are handled by the service rather than staying on your device. If you're merging contracts, splitting bank statements or compressing tax forms, you may prefer a tool that never receives them at all.

This guide compares iLovePDF's free plan with PrivyForge's PDF tools, which run entirely in your browser.

## iLovePDF's free plan (as of October 26, 2026)

According to iLovePDF's [pricing page](https://www.ilovepdf.com/pricing):

- The free **Basic** plan offers "limited document processing"; Premium offers "unlimited document processing."
- **Files per task** on the free plan range from 1 to 25 depending on the tool — for example 25 for Merge and 2 for Compress.
- **File size per task** ranges from 15 MB to 400 MB depending on the tool — for example 100 MB for Merge and 200 MB for Compress.
- The free plan shows ads; Premium removes them.
- **Premium** is listed at US$5 per month billed annually or US$9 billed monthly, and adds features such as all OCR tools and regional file processing.

## What iLovePDF does that PrivyForge doesn't (yet)

An honest comparison has to say this: iLovePDF has far more tools. Its pricing page lists, among others, Office-to-PDF conversion, PDF to Word, Excel and PowerPoint, OCR, signing and redaction. PrivyForge currently covers the most common jobs — merge, split, compress, compress to a size target, images to PDF, PDF to JPG, PDF to text (with OCR for scanned pages), signing and form filling — and does them privately. If you need Office conversion or redaction today, iLovePDF (or a desktop PDF editor) is the better fit.

## Why "no upload" matters for PDFs

PDFs tend to be exactly the documents you'd least like to hand to a third party: leases, payslips, medical letters, passports, tax returns. A server-based tool has to receive a full copy of the file to work on it, even if it deletes the file later. PrivyForge uses open-source libraries (pdf-lib and pdf.js) that run in your browser, so the document is only ever read into your device's memory.

You can check this yourself: open a PrivyForge PDF tool, wait for it to load, turn off Wi-Fi, and use it.

## How to merge PDFs privately

1. Open [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf/) and drop in your files.
2. Drag them into the order you want (or use Alt + arrow keys).
3. Click **Merge & download**.

For other jobs: [Split PDF](/split-pdf/) extracts pages or ranges, and [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf/) shrinks files for email and upload forms.

## Common questions

**Is it really free with no limits?**
Yes. Because PrivyForge doesn't pay for servers to process your files, it doesn't need to ration them. It's supported by ads and labeled affiliate links.

**Can I merge password-protected PDFs?**
Not yet — remove the password in your PDF reader first.

**Will my bookmarks and form fields survive a merge?**
Page content is copied exactly. Document bookmarks and interactive form fields from the source files may not carry over.

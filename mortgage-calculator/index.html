How this mortgage calculator works

Your monthly principal and interest (P&I) uses the standard fixed-rate formula: M = P × r / (1 − (1 + r)−n), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of payments. Property tax, homeowners insurance and HOA dues are added on top, because most lenders collect taxes and insurance through escrow.

PMI

If your down payment is under 20%, conventional lenders usually charge private mortgage insurance. We estimate it from the annual rate you enter and drop it automatically once your balance reaches 78% of the home's original price — the point where US federal law requires lenders to cancel it.

Extra payments

Every extra dollar goes straight to principal, so less interest accrues every month after. Add an amount above to see the total interest saved and how many months sooner you'd own your home.

Frequently asked questions

What's included in "monthly payment"?

Principal and interest, plus your extra payment, property tax, insurance, HOA and PMI (if any). The KPI cards break each part out.

Is a 15-year or 30-year mortgage better?

A 15-year loan has a higher monthly payment but typically a lower rate and far less total interest. A 30-year keeps payments lower and flexible. Try both terms above, or keep the 30-year and add an extra payment to see a middle path.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. All calculations run in your browser. Your numbers only appear in the page URL so you can bookmark or share a scenario.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are estimates for planning. Your lender's Loan Estimate will show exact figures.

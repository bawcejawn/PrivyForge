About PrivyForge

PrivyForge started from a simple frustration: to compress a photo or merge two PDFs, the most popular free tools ask you to upload your files to their servers — then limit how many you can process before asking you to pay.

Modern browsers are powerful enough to do this work themselves. PrivyForge uses WebAssembly builds of proven open-source libraries (MozJPEG, libwebp, libavif, OxiPNG, pdf.js and pdf-lib) so every tool runs on your own device. Your files are never uploaded, which also means we can offer the tools without artificial limits.

Our principles

  • Your files stay yours. Tools process files locally. We never receive them.
  • Free means free. No daily caps, no watermarks, no sign-up for core tools.
  • Honest monetization. We show ads and label affiliate links clearly. We'll never sell your data.
  • Credit where due. PrivyForge is built on outstanding open-source software. Thank you to its maintainers.

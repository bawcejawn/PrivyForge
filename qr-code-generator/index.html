Why "free" QR codes from other sites stop working

Many QR sites create dynamic codes that point to their own redirect server. After a trial ends, the redirect is switched off and every printed menu, flyer or business card stops working until you pay. PrivyForge creates static codes: your link or data is encoded directly into the pattern, so it works forever, with no middleman tracking who scans it.

Tips for codes that scan reliably

  • Keep strong contrast: a dark code on a light background.
  • Print at least 2 × 2 cm (0.8 × 0.8 in); bigger for posters scanned from a distance.
  • Leave the margin ("quiet zone") around the code.
  • Use higher error correction if the code may get scratched or have a logo placed over it.
  • Always test-scan before printing.

    How to split a PDF

    1. Add your PDF.
    2. Choose a mode. Type ranges like 1-3, 8, 10- (a trailing dash means "to the end").
    3. Download. One result downloads as a PDF; several come as a ZIP.

    Why split PDFs locally?

    Splitting is often done to share part of a sensitive document: one page of a lease, a single bank statement, a section of a medical report. Uploading the whole file to a third party to do that defeats the purpose. PrivyForge splits files entirely in your browser, with no page or size limits.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does splitting reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied exactly as they are; nothing is re-rendered or re-compressed.

    Can I reorder pages?

    Use "Extract pages into one PDF" with the order you want, e.g. 3, 1, 2. Pages are added in the order listed.

    Is there a page limit?

    No. Documents with thousands of pages work; speed depends on your device.

Where to start

  1. Find money you're already losing. Export a few months of transactions from your bank as CSV and run the Subscription Finder. It lists every recurring charge and what it costs per year.
  2. Point that money at your most expensive debt. The Debt Payoff Planner compares the avalanche (highest rate first) and snowball (smallest balance first) methods and shows your debt-free date.
  3. Check your biggest loan. If you have a mortgage, see whether a refinance pays for itself before you'd move, or what extra payments do in the mortgage calculator.
  4. Plan the next big decision. Use the loan calculator before financing a car, or rent vs buy before house hunting.

Example: what an extra $100 a month does

Two of these free money tools, using their default numbers, show how much a small change can matter:

  • On a $25,000 car or personal loan at 7.9% for 5 years, the payment is $505.71. Adding $100 a month pays it off 11 months early and saves $1,074.13 in interest.
  • With four debts and a $900 monthly budget, the avalanche method is debt-free in 2 yr 10 mo. Raising the budget to $1,000 gets there in 2 yr 6 mo and cuts interest from $4,612 to $3,899.

Found $100 a month in forgotten subscriptions? Those are the kinds of places to send it.

How accurate are these calculators?

Loan schedules follow the convention US lenders use on statements: the monthly payment is rounded to the cent, each month's interest is rounded on the remaining balance, and the final payment is adjusted to clear the loan. The engine is tested against published examples, including a Consumer Financial Protection Bureau sample loan and the Texas Instruments BA II Plus guidebook.

They're still estimates. Your lender's disclosures and statements are the final word.

Is my financial information stored?

No. Calculations run on your device. Calculator inputs are kept in the page address only so you can bookmark or share a scenario, and bank CSV files are read in memory and never sent anywhere. You never need to connect a bank account or create a login.

Frequently asked questions

Can I share a scenario with my partner?

Yes. Calculator inputs are saved in the page address, so copy the link and send it. Whoever opens it sees the same numbers.

Do these work outside the US?

The math works for any fixed-rate loan in any currency; results are shown in dollars. Mortgage extras such as PMI and the tax notes follow US conventions.

Is this financial advice?

No. These tools give estimates to help you plan. For big decisions, compare official lender disclosures or talk to a qualified adviser.

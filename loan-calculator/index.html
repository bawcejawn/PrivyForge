How loan payments are calculated

Most consumer loans are fully amortizing: each payment is the same, but early payments are mostly interest and later payments are mostly principal. The chart above shows that crossover. Enter the APR from your offer to compare lenders on equal terms.

Frequently asked questions

Does paying extra really save money?

Yes, as long as your loan has no prepayment penalty. Extra payments reduce principal immediately, which lowers every future interest charge.

Can I use months instead of years?

Enter a fraction: 6 months = 0.5, 18 months = 1.5.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are estimates; your lender's disclosures are authoritative.
